Eight subjects, five levels, built for early homeschooling years.

The curriculum spans foundation readiness through early primary, covering academics alongside life skills, creativity, and physical development — not just reading and maths.

Levels

Levels are indicative, not strict age cut-offs — every child moves through them at their own pace.

Curriculum levels with indicative age and description
Level Name Indicative age Focus
L1 Foundation 1 ≈ 4–5 Early readiness: sounds, counting to 20, following simple instructions
L2 Foundation 2 ≈ 5–6 Blending, number sense to 20, simple measurement and sorting
L3 Early primary 1 ≈ 6–7 Reading simple sentences, operations within 10, tens and ones, money
L4 Early primary 2 ≈ 7–8 Fluent reading, standard units, place value to 100
L5 Early primary 3 ≈ 8–10 Extended writing, multi-step problems, inquiry

Subjects

English Language & Literacy

Phonics, reading, comprehension, writing

Mathematics

Number, measurement, money, shape

Home & Regional Languages

Listening, speaking, script recognition in the family's chosen language

Science & Nature

Observation, living things, materials, inquiry

Social & Environmental Studies

Family and community, maps, environment, culture

Life Skills & Independence

Self-care, safety, feelings, time

Creative Arts & Music

Drawing, craft, rhythm, drama

Physical Development & Health

Gross/fine motor, hygiene, nutrition

Sample activities

A few examples of what a scheduled activity looks like in practice.

Sample curriculum activities
Activity Subject Level Duration Summary
Build a paper rocket Creative Arts & Music L2 30 min Follow a plan to fold and tape a rocket.
Rhythm clap-along Creative Arts & Music L2 10 min Clap a steady beat along with a song.
Phonics: short vowels English Language & Literacy L2 15 min Blend short-vowel words and match them to pictures.
Story retell in 3 pictures English Language & Literacy L3 15 min Put the story pictures in order and tell the story.
Feelings faces Life Skills & Independence L2 10 min Name feelings from faces and talk about them.

This is a small sample of the growing activity library — more subjects, levels, and activities are added regularly.
