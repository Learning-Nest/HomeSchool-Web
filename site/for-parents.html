You're in control. Your child is protected.

HomeSchooling is built on one rule: a parent or guardian is always the account holder, always gives consent, and always stays in the loop. Here's exactly what that means.

How we protect your child

Verifiable guardian consent

Before any child profile is created or any child data is processed, we collect verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. No activity happens for a child until that consent is on record.

No child accounts

Children don't create logins, don't set passwords, and don't manage their own account. Every session runs under the guardian's account, with the guardian present or in control of access.

No ads, ever

HomeSchooling does not show advertising to families and does not sell attention. There is no ad network, no sponsored content, and no third-party ad script anywhere in the product.

No tracking of children

We do not run behavioural tracking, profiling, or targeted-advertising technology against a child's activity. Data collected about a child's learning is used only to show that family its own progress — nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Data handling aligned with the DPDP Act

Children's personal data is treated as sensitive by default: minimal collection, clear purpose, and a guardian's right to review, correct, export, or delete it at any time through account settings.

Media stays opt-in

Photo or video evidence in an activity is always optional and clearly marked as requiring consent before it's captured — never a default, never required to complete an activity.

Questions we expect you'll ask

Who can see my child's data?

Only guardians on the family account, and, where your family chooses to add one, an educator or coach role you explicitly grant access to. Our own staff access is limited, logged, and only for support or safety purposes.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Account settings include a request to export or delete your family's data. Deletion removes or anonymises records in line with the DPDP Act and our data retention policy.

Does my child need their own device?

No. HomeSchooling is designed to be used together, on a parent's device, with the parent guiding or supervising the session.

More detail

For the full legal text on what we collect and why, see our privacy notice. If you have a specific safety or privacy question, we'd rather hear it directly — contact us.
