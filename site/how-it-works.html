A simple rhythm: plan, do, reflect.

HomeSchooling isn't a curriculum that runs itself — it's a set of tools that make the homeschooling you're already doing easier to plan, easier to run day to day, and easier to look back on.

The weekly loop

  1. Plan the week

    Pick from a library of activities across subjects and levels, or use suggestions based on your child's goals and interests. Arrange them across the days that suit your family.

  2. Run today's activities

    Each activity has clear, short steps — an instruction, something for your child to do, and a simple parent check-in. No special training or equipment needed.

  3. Note what happened

    After an activity, mark how your child did — trying, with help, or independent — and optionally add a photo or a note to the learning journal.

  4. See the progress build up

    Completed activities and observations roll up automatically into a picture of skills learned, by subject, over weeks and months.

Real-life learning, not a screen substitute

Most activities happen away from a screen — in the kitchen, on paper, outdoors, in conversation. The app is there to plan the activity and record what happened, not to be the thing your child stares at all day.

The learning journal

Alongside planned activities, parents can log free-form observations at any time — "asked for water independently", "read a whole page unprompted" — and tag them to a skill. Over time this becomes a timeline of your child's real progress, not just what was scheduled.
