Homeschooling, organised — without losing the real-life part.

Plan your child's week, run hands-on activities together, and watch skills build up over time — all from a simple dashboard built for Indian homeschooling families.

See how it keeps your child safe How it works

Everything a homeschooling week needs, in one place

You already do the teaching. HomeSchooling takes care of the planning, the record-keeping, and the "what should we do today" — so more of your time goes to your child, not to spreadsheets.

Weekly planner

Build a day or a week from a library of activities across subjects, drag things around, and publish the plan your child follows.

Hands-on activities

Short, real-world activities — reading, maths, art, life skills — with clear steps a parent can run without any special training.

Progress you can see

Every completed activity and parent observation rolls up into a simple picture of what your child can do, and what's next.

Learning journal

Jot down what happened, tag it to a skill, add a photo if you want — a running record of your child's homeschooling journey.

Built around the family, not around the child alone

Your child never signs in on their own, never sees ads, and is never tracked. A parent or guardian stays in control of the account, the data, and what gets shared. Read more on our safety and privacy page.
