Who we are

HomeSchooling ("we", "us") provides a platform to help homeschooling families in India plan activities, run them with their children, and track progress. This notice explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

Data we collect

  • Account & family data: guardian name, email or phone, family and child profile details you provide (such as a child's name, age band, and learning goals).
  • Learning data: activities assigned and completed, parent observations, skill progress, and any journal entries or optional media you choose to add.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs (such as sign-in times and error reports) needed to operate and secure the service.

We do not knowingly collect data directly from a child without verifiable guardian consent, and children do not hold their own account or login.

Why we process it

  • To provide the planner, activities, and progress features you use.
  • To secure accounts and prevent misuse.
  • To improve the curriculum and product based on aggregated, de-identified usage patterns.
  • To communicate service updates, and, only with your consent, product news.

We do not use children's data for advertising, behavioural profiling, or targeted marketing, and we do not sell personal data.

Consent for children's data

Before we process any data relating to a child, we obtain verifiable consent from that child's parent or guardian, as required under the DPDP Act. A guardian may withdraw this consent at any time, which stops further processing of that child's data and triggers deletion or anonymisation in line with our retention practices.
